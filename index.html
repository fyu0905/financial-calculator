Companion to SUFE Corporate Finance, lectures 4–5. House rule: keep P/Y = C/Y = 1, type the periodic rate into I/Y (in percent), and let N be the number of periods. Cash outflows are negative.

Start with Example 0 (reset + P/Y = 1), then the present value, LiSi's loan, the monthly stream, the two remaining keys, and finally ZhangSan's car. TI guidebook

Examples

Starting an example resets the calculator. Click Step ▶ to advance one keystroke group at a time, or Run all to replay it.

Practice

Answers stay hidden. Work each one on the keypad or keyboard, then press Check. Either sign viewpoint is accepted, as long as it is consistent.
